This study aimed to develop an Io'I-based flood alert system for Narvacan, Sto.
Tomas, La Union. Specifically, the study sought to achieve the following objectives: 1)
develop a prototype of the loT-Based Flood Alert System adopting the agile methodology,
and 2) assess the system's level of acceptability using the User Technology Acceptability
Questionnaire. Throughout the study, descriptive and developmental research methods
were employed to accomplish its objectives. It focused on water level monitoring and an
early warning system via SMS that alerted individuals to potential flood events, giving time
to evacuate and abate the impact of flooding. The loT-Based Flood Alert System for
Narvacan, Sto. Tomas. La Union, was perceived as extremely high acceptance in terms of
system usefulness, information quality, and interface quality with a grand mean of6.87.

INTRODUCTION
Situation Analysis
Technological advancement has led the emergence of loT-Based solutions that
paved the way for a new chapter of innovation. This study embarked on a comprehensive
exploration of the 'loT-Based Flood Alert System', delving into its development,
functionality, and critical role in mitigating the devastating impact of floods. The loT-
Based system comprised various components, including sensors that monitored water
levels in real-time, a cloud-based platform that stored and processed the data, and a mobile
application that alerted users of the current flood situation while providing them with
evacuation routes and safety tips.
Cicek and Kantarci (2023) emphasized in disaster management, the massive
amount of heterogeneous data that was generated through ubiquitous sensors paved the
way for fast and informed decisions in the case of disasters. Utilization of the big "sensed"
data led to effective and efficient management of disaster situations to prevent human and
economic losses. The advancement of built-in sensing technologies in smart mobile
devices enabled crowdsourcing of sensed data, which was known as mobile crowdsensing
(MCS), highlighting the numerous benefits of incorporating teclmology into emergency
response, including better tracking and locating individuals needing assistance.
The study of Shah et al. (2018) proposed a flood warning system that could detect
the water level and measure the speed of the rise of water level. To give the society an
earlier notification to evacuate before the water rose to the dangerous level, the


2
measurement results were sent as alerts to mobile pbones through Short Message Service
(SMS). This project was designed on the loT-based platform, where data from the sensor
was collected at the mini-processor, and alerts were generated and transmitted as SMS to
smartphones.
The motivation behind the development of a flood monitoring system arose from
the critical need of communities residing in flood prone areas, local disaster management
agencies, or even government bodies responsible for public safety. Through surveys and
discussions, it was evident that such stakeholders sought an efficient system that could
provide timely flood alerts, enabling them to make informed decisions and take necessary
precautions (Byali et aI., 2022).
As climate-related challenges became increasingly evident, the need for effective
flood monitoring and alert systems became more crucial than ever. This research paper
delved into the development of flood detectors using loT Arduino technology, aiming to
provide a comprehensive solution to monitor flood situations and send timely alerts via
text messages (Liang et aI., 2023).
Likewise, the reports from reputable agencies consistently highlighted the urgency
of improved flood monitoring and alert systems. The escalating frequency of extreme
weather events, often linked to climate change, further accentuated the relevance of this
contemporary issue (Radhika et al., 2022). The researchers aimed to contribute to resolving
this issue by developing a practical loT -based solution that not only identified flood
situations through data analysis but also communicated the potential risks effectively,
ultimately minimizing the impact of floods on lives and properties.

According to Sharma (2023), it was explored that the existing dam reservoir
monitoring systems in India heavily relied on water gauges to identify potentially
hazardous water levels indicative of floods. In addition, these systems faced challenges due
to the remote locations of dams, resulting in high costs and significant human resources
needed for continuous monitoring and maintenance. However, the primary objective of the
study was to address these issues by offering a reliable, cost-effective, low-maintenance,
and energy-efficient approach to intelligent flood detection and dam reservoir monitoring
(Keith, 2018).
On the other hand, the advancements in computer systems revolutionized flood risk
management, reducing manual efforts and enhancing coordination. With climate change
and socioeconomic developments escalating flood severity, recent studies showed a
transition from traditional flood control to a more adaptive and resilient approach. A
comprehensive bibliornetric analysis spanning from 2000 to 2021 revealed this shift in
flood research trends, emphasizing a move from mitigation-focused strategies to adaptive
measures and, ultimately, resilient and sustainable disaster coping plans. The integration
of resilience into risk management frameworks emerged as a crucial strategy, underscoring
the importance of informed decisions and proactive measures throughout the disaster
management continuum (Wang et al., 2022).
The rapid pace of technological change presented challenges for users, notably in
specialized fields such as coastal management and disaster preparedness. However, it
concurrently engendered transformative advancements. The transition from manual,
resource-intensive processes to computer-assisted monitoring and decision-making

addressing intricate problems, emphasizing the paramount importance of adaptability and

data visualization assisted in efficiently identifying afflicted regions (Advanced
Environmental Monitoring, 2024). These technological advancements significantly
enhanced people's capacity to respond to coastal disasters, sparing lives and minimizing
property loss.

Floods are undoubtedly one of the most devastating natural calamities that ravage
millions worldwide yearly. The extent of the damage they could cause ranges from the
destruction of infrastructure and property to the loss of human lives and agriculture. Due
to its geographic location, climate, and topography, the Philippines is especially prone to
floods. In La Union the areas that are highly susceptible to flooding include Barangay
Narvacan, Sto. Tomas, La Union, a coastal municipality in the northern part of Luzon
Island. The inhabitants of this region faced constant threats from heavy rainfall, storm
surges, and river overflow during the rainy season by Muaz (2021).
During an interview, Barangay Captain Ricardo Collado ofNarvacan, Sto. Tomas,
La Union, expressed concern over the recurring high tide events that occurred quarterly in
the area. These high-tide incidents required prompt action to mitigate potential risks to
coastal communities. He also discussed the impact of high tides on the community
biannually, with a seasonal occurrence in the months of February to April and from
September to December. Furthermore, weekly instances of high tides extended over three


17
nights and encompassed at least two days. It was imperative to address these patterns
comprehensively to ensure the resilience and safety of the community (Collado, 2023).
However, the barangay was intricately divided into seven sectors, and recent data
unveiled a concerning trend. Sectors five to seven, inhabited by 233 individuals, grappled
with the impact of severe high tides, whi Ie sectors one to four, housing 338 out of the total
571 residents, faced moderately high tides. The tangible repercussions on the affected
population's lives and livelihoods underscored the urgent need for intervention. Delving
into the broader context, the Municipality of Santo Tomas had a historical backdrop of
contending with the challenges posed by tidal fluctuations, a recurrent phenomenon in this
coastal locale. The residents ofNarvacan, Sto. Tomas, La Union had traditionally relied on
manual monitoring techniques, encompassing recording high tide date and time, predicting
water levels, measuring water levels, and discerning the conclusion of high tide.
Additionally, they employed a calendar-based method to track tidal cycles and a manual
process to monitor rising water levels. The vulnerability of the barangay to flooding was
accentuated by its low-lying topography and close proximity to rivers and the sea (Collado,
2023). In light of these complexities, implementing strategic measures became paramount
to mitigate the impacts, ensuring the protection and resilience of the community against
imminent harm.
Due to the aforementioned scenario and problems encountered by the residents, the
researchers developed the loT-Based Flood Alert System for Narvacan, Sto. Tomas, La
Union. To avert such situations, it was important to monitor and receive timely alerts about
the water level situation. The system would assist in evaluating emergency preparedness

and trace the affected households in the focus area from the':seven sectors of the said
barangay. The function of this system provided an alert message through SMS informing
the residents of the status of water level in the vicinity and to evacuate their place if
necessary.
The developed loT-Based Flood Alert System stood as a comprehensive solution,
offering real-time data on water levels. This innovative system went beyond conventional
monitoring technologies by incorporating features like text messages and calls,
establishing a robust alert system that significantly enhanced coastal risk management and
emergency response. The prototype, equipped with advanced sensors and integrated with
weather stations, served as a proactive too] for tracking water levels, tides, and weather
conditions. The system's core functionality lay in its ability to deliver timely alerts and
warnings to 30 participants, ensuring heightened preparedness in the face of high tide or

the Municipality of Santo Tomas but also envisioned extending its benefits to other flood-
prone areas in the Philippines, particularly empowering local communities in the province
to mitigate the impact of coastal risks through effective communication networks and
technologically-driven response mechanisms.

Chapter 4
SUMMARY, CONCLUSIONS AND RECOMMENDATIONS
Summary
This research aimed to develop an loT-Based Flood Alert System for Narvacan,
Sto. Tomas, La Union. The motivation behind the study was the vulnerability of the area
to flooding due to its low-lying topography and proximity to rivers and the sea. The system
was designed to monitor water levels in real-time and provide timely alerts to residents
through SMS, enabling them to evacuate or take necessary precautions in case of potential
flood events.
The researchers employed descriptive and developmental research methods,
utilizing Agile methodology to guide the system's development. The system components
included Arduino Uno, ultrasonic sensors, ESP-Ols WiFi Module, Active Buzzer, and a
web-based interface including C/C++, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, PHP, and MySQL, for an
intuitive user interface and efficient data management. The system was designed to analyze
water levels, predict potential floods, and send SMS alerts to residents.
To assess the system's acceptability, the researchers administered the User
Technology Acceptability Questionnaire (UTAQ) to a sample of30 respondents, including
9 barangay council officials and 21 residents from each of the seven sectors of Barangay
Narvacan, Sto. Tomas, La Union. The user survey for the loT -Based Flood Alert System
painted a very positive picture.


92
The following are the sal ient findings of the study:
1. Through an interview questionnaire, the researchers were able to ascertain the
prototype techniques used in the development of the system, which enabled
them to successfully develop an loT -based flood alert system using the Agile
methodology model.
2. The IoT-Based Flood Alert System received overwhelming endorsement from
users, with a grand mean score of 6.87 (Strongly Agreed). This reflected
extremely higb acceptance across all categories: usefulness (6.87), information
quality (6.83), and interface quality (6.82). These scores indicated users found
the system both valuable and informative, with nearly 98% rating it as such,
and user-friendly and easy to interact with, with nearly 97% agreement. This
strong user perception suggested the system had high potential for successful
community adoption.
Conclusions
Based on the findings of the study, the following conclusions were drawn:
I. The Agile Methodology proved suitable for the development of the loT-Based
Flood Alert System. This software development utilized components that were
