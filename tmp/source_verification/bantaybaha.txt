Abstract 
 
The advancements in technology have resulted to a new 
form of monitoring the flood, the use of Internet of Things. 
Internet of Things(IoT) can now connect every device                 
imaginable with the right devices and software. It can be a 
person with a heart monitor implant, a farm animal, an 
automobile that has built -in sensors to alert the driver 
when tire pressure is low, a device that checks the volume 
of water in the farm through the use of sensors and many 
more. The researchers chose “BantayBaha: Flood                      
Monitoring System with SMS Notification” to assist on a 
barangay called Virgen Delas Flores which is located in 
Baliwag, Bulacan. The method used by the barangay               
officials to monitor the flood is by using measurement bars 
on their posts. Integrating the Flood Monitoring System to 
their barangay would really help them be more prepared 
and lessen their burden because the device itself will send 
an SMS notification to the residents of the barangay                
indicating the water level of the flood and the data will be 
recorded and saved to the website .a realistic perspective 
of the university campus.

Project Context 
 
 The project seeks to integrate an innovative flood 
monitoring system with SMS notification. The proponents 
proposed a flood monitoring device that will monitor the  
water level of the place and when it reaches a certain level it 
will automatically send a text message using SMS notification 
to the people to alert them. The main objective of this study is 
to introduce a flood monitoring system, to help the citizens 
be prepared and knowledgeable whenever there is flood. 1.3 
PURPOSE AND DESCRIPTION OF THE PROJECT The project 
proposed by the researchers is an Flood Monitoring System 
with SMS notification for Virgen Delas Flores (VDF). The             
project also includes user management system. The Internet 
of Things allows the users to monitor the flood online.                    
Moreover, it is fully customized according to the needs of the 
barangay which also means that more features can be added 
or be modified in the future.  
 
Objectives of the Project  
 
 The main objective of this project is to create a              
technologically advanced flood monitoring device that can 
send SMS notification to the people regarding the water level 
status of their barangay. Specific Objectives: The following 
are the objectives of BantayBaha: Flood Monitoring System: 
1. To monitor the water level of the flood. 2. To send an SMS 
notification update to the residents of Virgen Delas Flores
(VDF), Baliwag Bulacan. 3. To inform the residence of VDF for 
evacuation in case it reaches the critical level using the LED 
light signals. 4. To have the capability to log the changes in 
water level. 5. To be able to add and delete users from the 
database.  
 
Scope and Limitation 
  
 The BantayBaha: flood monitoring device can detect 
the water level of the flood. After detecting the water level of 
the area it will automatically send sms notification to the   

62 
 
 
residents of Brgy. Virgen Delas Flores, Baliwag, Bulacan. The 
device can create a logsheet that contains the water level, 
time, and phone number who received the text message. This 
logsheet will be displayed in the website. There will be a user 
interface to add or remove users from the database. This   
project does not use solar power supply as energy source. 
The system cannot determine the temperature of the flood. 
The device cannot control or remove the flood of the area 
affected.  
 
Significance of the Project 
  
 Residents of Brgy. Virgen Delas Flores, Baliwag,          
Bulacan This project will help the residents to be informed of 
the upcoming flood and to prepare for evacuation if                      
necessary. Barangay Officials This project will help the                 
barangay officials to be more cautious and alerted in terms of 
monitoring the flood level of their barangay. Schools This 
project will help the schools to react or suspend the classes 
immediately whenever they receive alert messages from the 
flood monitoring device.
