P0010 [Heading 1] 1. Document Overview
P0011 [Heading 2] 1.1 Purpose
P0012 [No style] This Software Requirements Specification (SRS) defines the proposed functional, data, technical, safety, and validation requirements for FloodSense before software development begins. It replaces implementation claims in the earlier feature specification with a planning baseline that can be reviewed by the research team, technical adviser, thesis adviser, and prospective institutional data providers.
P0013 [Heading 2] 1.2 Proposed Product Scope
P0014 [No style] FloodSense will be a scenario-based Android application intended to help Bacoor City residents understand generalized flood susceptibility and prepare before a flood event. The proposed core will use a rule-based Expert System, a PostgreSQL/PostGIS database, a backend application programming interface (API), and a Flutter mobile client. The system will not function as an official forecast, live warning service, risk model, or independent evacuation authority.
P0015 [Heading 2] 1.3 Planning Status
P0017 [Heading 2] 1.4 Governing Assumptions
P0018 [Normal] The project will prioritize a working, explainable Expert System before optional conversational AI.
P0019 [Normal] Results will be described as scenario-based susceptibility estimates rather than real-time safety declarations.
P0020 [Normal] Geographic precision will not exceed the precision of the approved spatial and historical data.
P0021 [Normal] Institutional collaboration, data release, expert validation, and evacuation-center accuracy will be treated as pending until documented.
P0022 [Normal] Provisional demonstration data may be used during development only when clearly labelled and excluded from final accuracy claims.

P0055 [Heading 2] 4.2 DOST-ASTI Rainfall Data Planning Note
P0056 [No style] DOST-ASTI has stated that most stations were installed around 2012 and that data may be provided from station installation until the last transmission. The provider stated that records before 2023 are generally transmitted at 15-minute intervals and records from 2023 onward at 10-minute intervals. Rainfall and water-level variables may be available depending on station type and location. Exact Bacoor or nearest-station coverage, file structure, units, missing periods, quality flags, and licensing remain to be confirmed.
P0057 [Normal] The raw 10-minute and 15-minute observations will be preserved unchanged.
P0058 [Normal] Processing will calculate comparable accumulations such as 1-hour, 3-hour, 6-hour, 12-hour, and 24-hour totals after units and accumulation behavior are verified.
P0059 [Normal] Station observations will not be treated as representative of all Bacoor areas without geographic and expert justification.
P0060 [Normal] Rainfall records will not independently determine susceptibility; they must be paired with flood-event evidence and expert review.
P0061 [Heading 2] 4.3 Data Quality and Provenance Requirements
P0062 [Normal] Every dataset shall record its provider, request/reference number, date received, license, permitted use, geographic coverage, temporal coverage, units, and update date.
P0063 [Normal] Original source files shall be preserved separately from cleaned or derived data.
P0064 [Normal] Missing, estimated, corrected, or rejected values shall be explicitly flagged.
P0065 [Normal] No provisional or unverified dataset shall be presented to users as official or current.
P0066 [Normal] Every rule shall link to its evidence sources and expert review record.
P0067 [Heading 1] 5. Expert System Requirements
P0068 [Heading 2] 5.1 Selected Expert System Type
P0069 [No style] FloodSense will use a rule-based production system represented through decision tables and evaluated using forward chaining. The engine will begin with facts obtained from the user scenario, geographic lookup, and the approved zone profile. It will then identify matching rules, resolve applicable conflicts, and return an explainable conclusion. This approach is proposed because it is transparent, reviewable by subject-matter experts, and does not depend on a large labelled training dataset.
P0070 [Heading 2] 5.2 Expert System Components
P0072 [Heading 2] 5.3 Proposed Fact Model
P0073 [Normal] Resolved geographic zone and parent barangay.
P0074 [Normal] Rainfall intensity category and applicable official threshold version.
P0075 [Normal] Rainfall duration or verified accumulation interval.
P0076 [Normal] Static baseline susceptibility or approved zone profile attributes.
P0077 [Normal] Historical flood-frequency/depth category where supported.
P0078 [Normal] Rule-set version and evaluation timestamp.
P0079 [Heading 2] 5.4 Knowledge Acquisition and Rule Construction
P0080 [Normal] Define the geographic units supported by available evidence.
P0081 [Normal] Create historical cases linking rainfall observations to dated and located flood records.
P0082 [Normal] Present the cleaned cases and maps to designated experts through a structured elicitation form or workshop.
P0083 [Normal] Record proposed conditions, classifications, exceptions, uncertainty, and preparedness implications.
P0084 [Normal] Convert the accepted combinations into a decision table.
P0085 [Normal] Assign a rule identifier, source links, reviewer, approval status, priority, confidence, version, and effective date.
P0086 [Normal] Test the proposed rules against historical or expert-labelled cases not used during construction.
P0087 [Normal] Activate only rules that meet the approved validation and sign-off criteria.
P0088 [Heading 2] 5.5 Forward-Chaining Inference Procedure
P0090 [Heading 2] 5.6 Conflict Resolution and Uncertainty
P0091 [Normal] A sub-zone rule shall take precedence over a barangay-wide fallback rule when both are approved and applicable.
P0092 [Normal] A rule with all required conditions satisfied shall take precedence over a more general rule.
P0093 [Normal] Only approved and active rules may produce public-facing classifications.
P0094 [Normal] The current effective version shall take precedence over superseded versions.
P0095 [Normal] An expert-assigned priority may be used only when its rationale is recorded.
P0096 [Normal] If equally applicable rules disagree, the system shall return Uncertain and shall not silently choose the more severe or more convenient output.
P0097 [Normal] If no rule applies, the system shall return Insufficient Data and display the limitation.

P0108 [Heading 1] 8. Proposed Technical Architecture
P0109 [No style] The proposed architecture separates the Android client from data storage and inference. The Flutter application will communicate with a secured backend API. The API will validate requests, resolve spatial locations, invoke the Expert System service, retrieve approved preparedness content, and access PostgreSQL/PostGIS. The mobile application will not connect directly to the database.
