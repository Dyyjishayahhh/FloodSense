P0008 [Heading 1] Purpose and Use of the Instruments
P0009 [Normal] This package contains two requirements gathering instruments for FloodSense. Questionnaire 1 is a survey for adult Bacoor City residents. Questionnaire 2 is a key informant interview and data availability guide for an authorized representative of the Bacoor Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office. The instruments gather current experiences, information needs, available institutional data, expert knowledge, preparedness guidance, and system requirements before full implementation.
P0010 [Normal] The questions examine present practice before the proposed FloodSense concept is disclosed. This order reduces the chance that respondents will shape their answers around the proposed application. The concept review appears near the end of each instrument so respondents can then judge value, risks, priorities, and appropriate limits.

P0473 [Heading 1] Part H FloodSense Concept Review and Feasibility
P0474 [Normal] Read this concept statement only after Parts A to G are complete. FloodSense is a proposed Android application supported by a Django web administration system. Residents select an approved rainfall intensity and duration and choose a supported area or manually position a pin. GeoDjango and PostGIS resolve the location, and a Rule-Based Expert System applies approved facts and rules to return an explainable susceptibility result. A dynamic map displays scenario-based classifications. A separate Decision Support System provides approved pre-event preparedness guidance, and verified evacuation centers may be displayed and ranked by straight-line distance. Authorized administrators maintain draft records, validate them, and publish approved versions. FloodSense does not provide a live flood forecast, current road-safety guarantee, or independent evacuation order.
