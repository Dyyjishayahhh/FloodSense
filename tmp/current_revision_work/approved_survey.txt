P0001 [Normal] Introduction / Panimula
P0002 [Normal] We are studying how Bacoor City residents receive and use flood-related information. We want to understand what currently works, what problems residents experience, and whether a proposed FloodSense application could be useful.

P0008 [Normal] Data Privacy Notice and Consent / Pahayag sa Pagkapribado at Pahintulot
P0009 [Normal] In accordance with Republic Act No. 10173, or the Data Privacy Act of 2012, the FloodSense research team will collect and process the personal information you provide, including your name, age, sex or gender, and general place of residence, only for this academic study on flood-information needs in Bacoor City. Participation is voluntary. You may skip a demographic question or stop answering at any time without penalty. Your responses will be kept confidential, accessed only by the research team and its academic advisers, reported in summary form whenever possible, retained only as long as necessary for the study and required academic recordkeeping, and then securely disposed of. Your information will not be sold or used for marketing. Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information, or withdraw your consent, by contacting the FloodSense research team through the survey administrator.
P0010 [Normal] Alinsunod sa Republic Act No. 10173, o Data Privacy Act of 2012, kokolektahin at gagamitin ng FloodSense research team ang personal na impormasyong ibibigay ninyo, kabilang ang inyong pangalan, edad, kasarian, at pangkalahatang lugar ng tirahan, para lamang sa akademikong pag-aaral na ito tungkol sa pangangailangan sa impormasyong may kaugnayan sa baha sa Bacoor City. Kusang-loob ang pagsali. Maaari ninyong laktawan ang isang tanong sa demograpiko o huminto anumang oras nang walang kaparusahan. Pananatiling kumpidensiyal ang inyong mga sagot, at tanging ang research team at mga academic adviser nito ang magkakaroon ng access. Ilalahad ang mga resulta sa pinagsama-samang paraan hangga't maaari, itatago lamang ang datos habang kailangan para sa pag-aaral at sa kinakailangang academic recordkeeping, at pagkatapos ay ligtas na buburahin o sisirain. Hindi ibebenta o gagamitin sa marketing ang inyong impormasyon. Alinsunod sa umiiral na batas, maaari kayong humiling na makita, iwasto, o ipabura ang inyong impormasyon, o bawiin ang pahintulot, sa pamamagitan ng pakikipag-ugnayan sa FloodSense research team sa survey administrator.
