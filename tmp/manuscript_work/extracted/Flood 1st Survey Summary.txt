5. Proposed Solution Concept (Working Title)
Working Title: FloodSense: An Augmented Reality Mobile Application with Machine Learning-Based
Flood Susceptibility Mapping for Bacoor City
Based on the identified gaps, this study proposes developing a mobile application that provides
residents with location-specific flood susceptibility information for Bacoor City. Using a machine
learning model trained on rainfall data, elevation, and historical flood records, the app classifies flood
risk into four clear levels: Low, Moderate, High, and Very High - for each zone/ barangay in the city.
Residents open the app, set their own parameters — how much rain is expected and how long it will
fall — then point their phone camera at any flat surface. The app renders a color-coded flood
susceptibility map anchored to the ground through augmented reality, giving them an intuitive, visual
answer to the question: "Is my area at risk right now?"
