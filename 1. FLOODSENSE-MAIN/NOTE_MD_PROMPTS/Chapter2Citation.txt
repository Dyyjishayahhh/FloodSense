# Citation-style requirement

Mirror SURECUT’s dominant APA-style narrative citation pattern, also called author-prominent citation.

The author or responsible organization should normally be incorporated into the sentence, with the year immediately following the author or organization.

Preferred patterns include:

- `Santos et al. (2023) developed…`
- `Cruz and Reyes (2022) designed…`
- `According to the Office of Civil Defense (2024), …`
- `DOST-PHIVOLCS (2023) introduced…`
- `The study conducted by Santos et al. (2023) demonstrated…`
- `The researchers further explained that…`

Use narrative citations as the dominant form when introducing and discussing sources.

Do not make parenthetical citations the dominant style.

Avoid repeatedly writing paragraphs that end only with citations such as:

- `(Santos et al., 2023).`

Parenthetical citations may still be used when grammatically necessary, when multiple sources jointly support one statement, or when using a narrative citation would make the sentence unnatural.

Every major source and application must normally be introduced through a verified narrative citation.

Do not mention an author, institution, or year unless it has been verified from the original publication or an authoritative primary source.

# FloodSense scope protection

Do not convert features found in reviewed literature or applications into FloodSense capabilities.

Maintain these confirmed FloodSense boundaries:

- FloodSense is a scenario-based, pre-event flood-susceptibility assessment and preparedness-support system.
- The user selects or confirms a hypothetical rainfall scenario.
- The user explicitly requests an assessment.
- The Rule-Based Expert System is deterministic.
- The susceptibility assessment remains separate from DSS preparedness guidance.
- Foreground location is user-initiated.
- Background GPS tracking is not supported.
- Manual location selection remains necessary.
- Ordinary administrators do not edit raw rules or algorithm logic.
- Technical Django Admin is separate from the ordinary custom Admin portal.
- Fixed-schema CSV export and import are confirmed requirements but are not currently implemented.
- The 47-barangay layer is an administrative reference layer rather than a flood-susceptibility dataset.
- Technical geometry validation is not equivalent to City verification.
- No approved operational susceptibility dataset is currently established.
- Provisional data are not official Bacoor City information.
- Survey evaluation remains incomplete.  
- Production deployment has not been established.
