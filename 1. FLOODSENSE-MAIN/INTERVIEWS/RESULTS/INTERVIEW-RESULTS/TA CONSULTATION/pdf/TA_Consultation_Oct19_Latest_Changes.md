# Chapter 1

## 1 Statement of the Problem

### 1.1 Make the first paragraph more general

**Consultation reference:** Page 1.

### What the TA said

The SOP starts too quickly with specific questions. The first paragraph should establish the biggest problem before the narrative moves toward the specific problems.

The TA confirmed the researcher's question about proceeding from general to specific. He described it as a story: explain the concern and lead the reader toward the questions without changing the existing SOP structure.

### Required revision

- Establish the broad concern first.
- Explain what the study is investigating at the general level.
- Then narrow toward the distinct specific problems.
- Keep the explanation connected and narrative.
- Introduce a particular problem question after providing the context necessary to understand it.

### Manuscript reference

The current SOP begins with the paragraph starting:

> Information from public advisories, barangay announcements, online sources, and personal contacts has different purposes and geographic detail.

That paragraph immediately presents the 40-person survey findings and asks about understandable, location-specific flood-susceptibility information.

The TA wants the overall problem established more clearly before moving into that first specific concern. His instruction is about the progression of the narrative, not removing all supporting survey evidence.

### Expected result

The reader should understand the overall issue before encountering the first narrow research question.

## 1.2 Put appendix references before the question

**Consultation reference:** Page 1.

### What the TA said

References such as “See Appendix Figure 2” must come before the question. The question should be the final part of the problem paragraph.

### Required revision

- Present the problem explanation.
- Present its evidence.
- Include the supporting appendix reference in that explanation.
- End with the research question.
- Move any explanation currently following the question to an appropriate earlier sentence.

### Manuscript reference

The current SOP places fishbone appendix references inside the research questions.

For example, its first question ends with:

> ...before heavy rainfall (see Appendix Figure 1. Fishbone Diagram of Location-Specific Flood Information Needs)?

Its final problem paragraph also has a sentence after the question explaining the status of the fishbone branches.

Both arrangements should be revised so that the supporting material precedes the question.

### Formatting example

The following illustrates the placement the TA described:

> Explanation of the problem and relevant findings, with the supporting appendix reference. Research question?

The example is a structural illustration, not proposed final SOP wording.

## 2 Scope and Limitations

### 2.1 Change the wording rather than conceal real limitations

**Consultation reference:** Pages 1 and 4.

### What the TA said

The limitations paragraph sounds as though the selected system or approach is inadequate compared with alternatives. That wording may cause panel members to question why the group chose its current method.

When the researcher asked whether specific limitations should still be included, the TA said they should. The problem was how those limitations were expressed.

### Required revision

- Retain the actual limitations.
- Explain the boundaries of the current scope.
- State what the selected algorithm and its parameters cover.
- Describe conditions that restrict use of the current system.
- Remove wording that introduces unrelated alternatives as though they are better approaches.

The TA's concern is the framing of the limitations. It is not permission to present an untested system as already effective.

## 2.2 Remove the unnecessary machine-learning comparison

**Consultation reference:** Page 1.

### What the TA said

He specifically questioned the mention of machine learning because it was not part of the current system. It made the discussion sound like a comparison between machine learning and the algorithm selected by the researchers.

### Required revision

Remove that comparison from the limitations discussion. Focus on the algorithm, parameters, functions, and restrictions actually within the system's scope.

### Manuscript reference

The current limitation paragraph includes:

> ...or use machine-learning prediction.

This is the specific kind of wording the TA objected to.

## 2.3 Describe limitations attached to the actual scope

**Consultation reference:** Page 1.

### Examples the TA gave

| Aspect | Boundary to explain |
| --- | --- |
| Internet connection | Whether Wi-Fi or mobile data is required to use the application. |
| Geographic coverage | Whether the supported location coverage is limited to Bacoor. |
| Location information or permission | What restrictions occur when the necessary location information is unavailable or access is not allowed. |
| Platform | Whether the resident application is limited to Android. |
| Evacuation-center information | Whether the system has center coordinates but cannot determine occupancy. |
| Assessment parameters | Which parameters are actually used by the selected algorithm. |

These are examples from the consultation. The revised wording must reflect what the application actually supports.

### Manuscript reference

The current paragraph beginning “However, the system is limited...” combines many exclusions and restrictions.

The TA wants it rewritten around the actual boundaries of the current system. The evacuation-center occupancy restriction is an example he accepted.

The draft also describes manual barangay selection, a map pin, and optional location access. When writing the location limitation, use those existing descriptions as a reference so that a general example about location permission is not converted into an inaccurate claim that the entire application requires GPS permission.

## 11 Establish Why the Functions Are Needed

**Consultation reference:** Page 3.

### What the TA said

The group must explain why users need particular functions and identify the survey or interview outputs from which those functions were derived.

When the researcher asked about another survey, the TA agreed and then referred to the basis available in previous interviews and surveys.

### Required outcome

The manuscript should show:

1. The problem identified in the data.

The point is to derive the proposed response from the problem reported by the participants.
